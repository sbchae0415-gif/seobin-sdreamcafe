📖 OUR STORY

My Dream Cafe Story

"Seobin's Dream Cafe was created from a personal wish to build a quiet, cozy haven where people can relax and find inspiration through artisan coffee."

What started as warm sketches in a manga notebook became a heartwarming reality. Every recipe in our cafe is handcrafted with premium beans, soft organic matcha, fresh fruits, and endless love to give you a comforting embrace.

✨ Handcrafted Artisan Quality: Our coffee beans are slowly roasted locally in small batches to preserve their velvety aroma and sweet notes.

🎨 Manga & Doodle Friendly: We offer free sketchbooks, cute stamps, and colored pencils on every table so guests can express their creativity freely.
